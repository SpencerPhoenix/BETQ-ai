DEFINITIONS & CONTROLS

What counts as evidence?

◇

Stake-based ROI = 100 × net profit ÷ total risked on graded bets. WIN, LOSS and PUSH are included; VOID and OPEN are excluded. Paper trades are not account-verified.

Raw price movement = 100 × (entry decimal odds ÷ closing decimal odds − 1). The same selection, period, and handicap must be compared.

No-vig closing benchmark = 100 × (entry decimal odds × margin-free closing probability − 1). Both sides of a matched closing market are required.

Actual CLV requires a documented filled entry and a time-stamped closing quote recorded immediately before kickoff. An illustrative pair is never verified CLV.

Not supported yet: audited real-money ROI, official closing-price coverage, or independently verified sportsbook fills. Results remain unavailable until those sources exist.
