# BETQ.ai v0.8 — Central Decision Authority and Conditional Bankroll Stress

## Why this release exists

Prior builds allowed a market-only WATCH discrepancy to offer automatic paper staging and a theoretical Kelly unit estimate, even while the five-stage risk agent issued PASS_NO_PROVEN_MARKET_EDGE. That was inconsistent and could cause users to mistake a cross-book price difference for a validated predictive edge.

v0.8 adds one central post-analysis decision authority in `src/decision-policy.mjs`. It runs on every `/api/slate` request **after** market analysis, independent projections and agent controls, using the same immutable quote snapshot. It requires no extra paid sportsbook API request.

### Authorization guarantees

- The market-only WATCH/PASS diagnostic remains intact as `rawMarketScreenStatus` for audit, but the UI calls WATCH **PRICE WATCH · NOT A BET**.
- Every opportunity carries `executionStatus=NOT_AUTHORIZED`, `executionAuthorized=false`, `paperStagingAuthorized=false`, `recommendedUnits=0`, and `theoreticalUnits=0`.
- The original scanner's hypothetical sizing is retained separately as `rawMarketScenarioUnits` for bounded audit, not a stake recommendation.
- Quote age, kickoff time, contract provenance, model validation and prior-season calibration are separate source/authority reasons.
- The server publishes `executionGate.bettingAuthorized=false` and `summary.actionableSelections=0` regardless of a positive market-only EV number.
- The browser's paper buttons are disabled and the click handler independently verifies the server agent plus execution authority. Existing previously saved paper tickets remain manually settleable as **unverified local paper history**. The forward evidence journal continues collecting honest pregame predictions separately.
- No real money betting, live sportsbook fills, or verified independent expected value model is authorized in this release.

## Bankroll stress — deliberately not a prediction

Historical source: 2022–2025 NFL replay's **879 hypothetical** 3%-modeled-EV selections, priced at *retrospective nflverse closing moneylines*, not authenticated executable entries. Source data are attributed to nflverse / Lee Sharpe (CC BY 4.0).

The `src/risk-stress.mjs` engine groups historical selections by season + week, resamples whole week groups with replacement, and generates 10,000 seeded bankroll paths per scenario. Each path samples 86 historical weekly blocks. The assumption maintains the *observed empirical selection-result distribution* and partially retains within-week clustering, but not regime changes, bet limits, bookmaker fills, injury vintages or future participant correlations.

Definition of practical loss threshold: an account **touches 50 of its starting 100 units at any time**. It is not literal zero-bankroll bankruptcy. Stake is a fraction of *current* hypothetical bankroll; no margin or leverage. The second scenario flips 2% of winning selections into losses as an explicit adverse assumption, not an estimated real-world probability.

| Conditional 10,000-path scenario | Median ending return | 5th percentile ending return | Frequency touching 50-unit threshold |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1% fractional stake, week-resampled historical pattern | −62.47% | −81.02% | 82.33% |
| Same, 2% adverse win-to-loss scenario | −67.90% | −83.76% | 90.51% |
| Half stakes (0.5%) | −37.46% | −55.58% | 18.76% |

These are **assumption-conditional Monte Carlo frequencies**, NOT independently validated real-world risk-of-ruin probabilities, actual profit/loss, or forward estimates of next season. In particular, a model already losing its price comparison cannot be repaired by altering unit sizing.

The artifact is generated by `scripts/run-risk-stress.mjs`, published at `data/risk/summary.json`, available through read-only `/api/risk/nfl`, and shown beside Historical Replay. It uses no additional Odds API credits. The random seed, scenario count, bankroll and threshold assumptions remain visible.

## Model promotion — future safety criteria

1. A model feature must have source-vintage-tracked inputs that were knowable when each forecast was recorded. No public final-result or historical closing-price feature may masquerade as a pregame signal.
2. Fit, calibration and testing seasons must be disjoint; include every year and every declined game to avoid selection-bias reporting.
3. Demonstrate independent incremental improvement against the matched-time market-only baseline on Brier and log loss with week-clustered uncertainty, not just winning percentage.
4. Validate selected-side probability calibration and true selection coverage against credible bookmaker prices **at the time the hypothetical decision would have been made**.
5. Collect a sufficiently sized genuinely prospective cohort with source-verified entry and closing quotes, explicit transaction fills and independently settled outcomes. Only then can a separately evaluated real-money edge, CLV or realized ROI be considered.
6. Run adversarial quality, rate-limit, stale-data, mixed-contract, cross-market and user-input tests. Keep real betting execution off until a separately authorized and audited capability exists.

## Quality checks

Dedicated tests include 10,000 randomized attempts to promote stale, malformed or hypothetical market signals past the central veto, seeded simulation reproducibility, practical-ruin boundary cases, invalid-input rejection, no-cost HTTP APIs and mobile UI provenance checks.
