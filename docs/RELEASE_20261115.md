## Operating boundaries

Ordinary public page loads, navigation and refreshes are cache-only and cannot spend odds
credits. Paid collection fails closed unless the operator token, dedicated PostgreSQL
ledger, verified balance and explicitly approved baseline-age policy are configured.
This release does not create a database, seed a balance, change secrets or authorize spend.
A healthy web response does not establish healthy collection.

Research remains unvalidated for wagering. Automatic paper staging and all real wagering
remain locked. Missing result qualification, current injury/starter verification, market
provenance, executable prices, verified CLV and realized ROI are not replaced with claims.
