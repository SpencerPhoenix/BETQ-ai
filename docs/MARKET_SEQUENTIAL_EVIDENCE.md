# BETQ.ai v0.11 — Prospective Market-Relative Sequential Evidence

## Current state: research only

BETQ now has a fixed, prospective scoring system that asks whether its earliest source-dated NFL forecasts consistently assign more probability to actual later results than the contemporaneous 3+ bookmaker no-vig consensus. It is **not a profitability, CLV, execution, or sportsbook price-availability test**. No automatic wagers or paper bets are enabled.

The research protocol was committed before evaluating the real 2026 forward journal: [PREREGISTERED_SEQUENTIAL_EVIDENCE.md](PREREGISTERED_SEQUENTIAL_EVIDENCE.md). This v1 rule uses one first eligible pregame forecast per game, the highest unambiguous independently recorded final-result revision, no outcomes from previous views, a fixed Elo/efficiency 50:50 mixture, and a research-only illustrative likelihood-ratio threshold of 20.

### Exact quantitative definition

For market home-win probability p, candidate model home-win probability q, and eventual home result y in {0,1}, the per-game likelihood ratio is:

LR = (q / p)^y * ((1−q) / (1−p))^(1−y)

Each candidate's log likelihood ratios accumulate only as new unique results settle. The fixed candidate mixture equals 0.5 × the Elo cumulative ratio plus 0.5 × the efficiency candidate cumulative ratio. Missing efficiency at the first matched snapshot contributes neutral q=p, not a retrospectively selected later version. Arithmetic is performed in log space.

Under the **unverified conditional null** that the no-vig sportsbook market probability exactly equals the event's true conditional win probability for each game (including dependencies), this nonnegative likelihood ratio is an e-process and the threshold of 20 has an illustrative at-most-5% ever-crossing property. Real sportsbook consensus is **not known to satisfy this null**, and weekly/team dependencies create further concerns. Therefore the ratio is a **conditional diagnostic**, not a calibrated real-world confidence or significance score.

To prevent overinterpretation, all public score differences, e-values and threshold status remain **WITHHELD until 30 separate, source-qualified and independently settled non-tie games are available**. Thirty is a descriptive reporting threshold, not proof of skill.

### Independently simulated mathematical integrity check

With 1,000 seeded synthetic worlds, 150 independent binary games per world, p_market=.50 and previsible fixed candidates q_Elo=.60, q_Efficiency=.40:

| Generative synthetic scenario | Worlds ever reaching mixture ratio 20 | Frequency |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Null: true p=.50 | 32 / 1,000 | 3.2% |
| Injected true signal: true p=.60 | 516 / 1,000 | 51.6% |

These percentages are **not** NFL backtests, financial returns, model improvements, or expected future outcomes. This is a toy world with known p and independent games. Do not compare its detection power directly against the previous 14,000-world ridge-model refitting study; the assumptions, candidate knowledge and number of outcome observations differ.

### Actual source data

As of the initial 2026 forward journal reviewed November 2, BETQ had **33 pregame snapshots across 17 tracked games, with only 1 settled game**. No meaningful prospective advantage or sequential ratio can be reported yet.

The existing derived-only public feed `/api/shadow/derived` now includes `sequentialMarketEvidence` and consumes **zero additional paid odds-provider requests**. It reads only BETQ's already stored source-frozen derived snapshots. The QB Latency Lab displays qualified sample counts and withholds model/market performance before the precommitted minimum. A source-hash audit is generated by `npm run audit:sequential` and automatically rechecked in GitHub Actions when the prospective journal updates.

### What would actually support profitability?

1. A new dedicated private database suitable for durable, access-controlled, licensed **bookmaker-identifiable, timestamped price snapshots**, including explicit provider time and collector first-receipt time.
2. Precisely identified full-game contracts and precommitted decision times, with clear evidence a price was actually offered at that moment, plus suspension/limits/attempted-fill states.
3. A narrow source-dated independent information event type (confirmed QB availability changes) and honest controls/placebo windows.
4. Sufficient genuinely new prospectively settled forecasts to demonstrate incremental accuracy over matched-time market baselines without multiple-model-selection bias.
5. Real fill price, stake, transaction evidence and independently verified settlement before any claim of realized ROI; same-contract authentic closing quotes before any CLV claim.

BETQ remains a research platform. The existing central hard veto, no-real-money-authorization and paper-staking lock stay intact.
